Privacy Policy

Effective: July 2026  ·  Contact: hello@trimwares.com

The short version: Trimwares Trace is a local-first tool. Your LLM prompts, responses, and session data never reach our servers. The only personal data we receive is your email address, used solely to deliver your license key.

1. What we collect

We collect the minimum possible:

  • Email address — provided at checkout via Lemon Squeezy, used only to deliver your Pro license key and send important account notices (e.g. key expiry).
  • Machine ID — a UUID generated on your device when you activate a Pro license. Stored in our license system to bind the key to up to 3 machines. We never see your device name, hardware details, or any other identifiers.
  • License key record — tier (free/pro), expiry date, and the email above. Stored in Cloudflare KV.

We do not collect:

  • Your LLM prompts or responses — these never leave your machine
  • The contents of .trimwares/session.jsonl
  • Which models you use or what you build
  • IP addresses beyond what Cloudflare logs transiently at the network layer

2. How your session data works

When you use the SDK, Trace writes a local metadata log to .trimwares/session.jsonl in your project directory. This file contains token counts, costs, attribution categories, latency, and cache results. It never contains prompt text, response text, or any content from your LLM calls.

This file is read only by the local dashboard at localhost:7777. It does not leave your machine at any point.

3. Payment processing

Payments are processed by Lemon Squeezy, which acts as the Merchant of Record for all Pro subscriptions. Lemon Squeezy handles all payment data, VAT, and tax compliance. We do not receive or store your card number, billing address, or payment details. Lemon Squeezy's privacy policy governs how they handle your payment information.

4. Email delivery

License keys are delivered by email via Resend. Your email address is passed to Resend solely for the purpose of sending the license key email. We do not use your email for marketing unless you separately opt in.

5. Infrastructure

License validation is handled by a Cloudflare Worker at license.trimwares.com. Cloudflare may process and transiently log request metadata (IP, timestamp, response code) in accordance with their privacy policy. We do not access or retain these logs.

6. Data retention

Your license record (email, tier, expiry, machine IDs) is retained for the duration of your subscription plus 90 days, after which it is deleted. You can request deletion at any time by emailing hello@trimwares.com.

7. Your rights

You can request access to, correction of, or deletion of any personal data we hold. Email hello@trimwares.com and we will respond within 5 business days.

8. Changes to this policy

If we make material changes, we will update the effective date above and notify active subscribers by email.

9. Contact

Questions about this policy: hello@trimwares.com
